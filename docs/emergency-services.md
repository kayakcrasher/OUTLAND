# Policio and Hospitalo de Verda

Explore's emergency services.

## Buildings and vehicles

- **Police station** (city kit `civic/police_station.glb`, Creator: "Police Station (modular,
  enterable, carport)"). It is one storey in white render over a navy base, with a glass
  entrance, a blue lamp, a public counter, two barred cells, a radio mast and a carport. The
  world kit puts one in **every town** (not the four small trailer hamlets by the highways:
  Ruldomaro, Vojrando, Pinejo and Sablovalo), facing a street on level ground near the centre. Island
  life staffs it as a police place, so it carries the POLICO sign and flag. A patrol car parks
  in front of each station, and Verda City has two.
- **Hospitalo de Verda** (city kit `civic/hospital.glb`), about 55 m from Verda City's police
  station. It has three storeys in white with blue ribbon windows and red crosses, an enterable
  ground-floor hall with reception and beds, and a drive-under ambulance bay with a canopy. Its
  sign reads "HOSPITALO DE VERDA · URĜA HELPO · AMBULANCOJ". **Three ambulances** park along the
  kerb in front.
- **Patrol car** (`police`): a navy and white sedan with a blue-and-white chequered band, a push
  bar, a cage and a roof light bar. **Ambulance** (`ambulance`): a white box ambulance with a
  green stripe, red crosses, light bars and rear doors. Both are generated by
  `tools/generate_emergency_vehicles.py`. Either can be stolen; a stolen unit is out of service.
- Residents and gang hit squads never take the services' vehicles.
- Vehicles treat a building model's whole bounds as solid, so nothing parks under the carport or
  the bay canopy. They are there for show.

## Police pursuit

While the player is wanted:

1. The nearest patrol cars drive out by road with lights and siren to where the player was last
   seen: one car per star, three at most.
2. A car that sees the player driving gives chase, straight at them.
3. When the player is on foot nearby, the car pulls up and its two officers get out. At one star
   they come unarmed to make the arrest. From two stars they are armed and shoot.
4. If the player drives off, the officers get back in and the chase resumes.
5. When the search is called off (arrest, the police lose the player, or the player dies), the
   cars drive home to their stations.

Officers out of the cars count for Justice: they keep the trail warm and can make the arrest.
Hurting one is reported at once, because they saw it.

## Clinics and ambulances

Hospitalo de Verda has three ambulances. Every other town has a **Kliniko**, a one-storey clinic
near its centre (`civic/clinic.glb`: red crosses over the door and on a roof sign, a reception
desk and three beds inside), with its own ambulance at the kerb. The compass shows the nearest one
as HOSPITAL or CLINIC.

Anyone down in the street is collected by the nearest free ambulance. That means a resident
with health under 60, a dead resident, or a fallen officer or paramedic.

1. The ambulance drives there with lights and siren. If the roads run out short of the patient,
   it drives straight at them for the last stretch rather than parking far away.
2. Two paramedics get out and go to the patient. They don't run from gunfire while they work.
3. The patient is loaded, the crew get back in, and the ambulance drives back to its own clinic or
   the hospital, with lights and siren if the patient is alive.
4. An ambulance (or patrol car) that can't find its way home tries fresh routes, then drives
   straight for home. It is never parked in the street and left there: if it is still stuck, it
   waits until the player has gone and is put back at its base.

The wounded are treated for 90 island minutes (`LifeSimulation::hospitalise`) at that clinic or
the hospital, then walk out of its door back into their day. The dead go to the morgue.

**When the player dies** in Explore and respawns, an ambulance has brought them in: they wake up
outside the nearest clinic or the hospital to where they fell, never at home
(`EmergencyServices::nearest_medical`).

## Sound

The siren is a 4 s wail loop (`assets/audio/weapons/siren.wav`, made by
`tools/generate_weapon_audio.py`). The nearest siren on the island plays, placed and panned like
a gunshot.

## Code

- `game::services::EmergencyServices`: the units, police dispatch and pursuit, the ambulance
  runs, the crew on foot (NpcSystem residents from `crew_base`), and `draw_light_bars`.
- `ai::steer_along`: road driving shared with the Battle Royale and Mafio bots.
- `Justice::witnessed` and `JusticeFrame::patrol_officers`; `LifeSimulation::hospitalise`.
- World kit: `emergency_services()` in `src/dev/VerdaWorldKit.cpp`.
- Tests: `outland_emergency_services_tests` covers:
  - a station in every town but the trailer hamlets, seven patrol cars and three ambulances;
  - one car per star, lights and siren, the car pulling up and armed officers getting out;
  - a pursuit when the suspect drives away;
  - the cars going home;
  - an ambulance collecting a wounded resident, the resident admitted to hospital, and the
    ambulance back at the hospital.

## Car damage (Explore)

Cars used to be wrecked far too easily. The island's own drivers (traffic, buses, gangs, police) took crash damage on every bump, a hatchback died to four rifle rounds, and wrecks never came back. Cars now behave like this:

- **Crashes** only damage the player's own car, and only above 32 km/h (9 m/s): 1.2 per m/s over that, at most 25 per crash. A really hard crash, over 58 km/h, also hurts the driver.
- **Bullets** take 35% as much as before. Most rounds pass through sheet metal, so a hatchback takes about a dozen rifle hits.
- **Wrecks** are towed home and repaired once the player has been more than 70 m from both the wreck and its home for 90 s (`VehicleSystem::tow_seconds` and `tow_distance`).
- **Saves:** the save file now loads the whole fleet. It used to refuse anything over 128 cars and then stop saving altogether.
