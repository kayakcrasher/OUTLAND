# OUTLAND Vehicles

OUTLAND will eventually support both ground vehicles and aircraft.

Ground vehicle systems may include:

- acceleration
- braking
- steering
- suspension
- health
- collision damage
- fuel
- enter/exit behavior

Aircraft systems may include:

- throttle
- pitch
- roll
- yaw
- lift
- landing
- aircraft health

Vehicle gameplay logic should remain separated from graphical models
whenever practical.
