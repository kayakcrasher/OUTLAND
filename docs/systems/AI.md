# OUTLAND AI

OUTLAND is primarily an offline game.

AI agents therefore replace many roles normally performed by human
players in an online battle royale.

AI development will be incremental.

Initial behavior:

- idle
- patrol
- detect player
- pursue player
- attack player

Later behavior:

- investigate sounds
- seek cover
- loot
- select weapons
- heal
- flee
- flank
- drive vehicles
- navigate towns
- operate across world chunks

AI simulation should support simplified behavior when agents are far
from the player.
