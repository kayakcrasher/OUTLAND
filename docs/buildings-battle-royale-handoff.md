# Real building collision, open windows, builder fixes and the first dressed towns

## What changed for players (Battle Royale first)

**Buildings are now played by their real shape, not a box.** Previously, every model building collided as a solid box: you couldn't walk through doorways, shoot through windows, or see through them. Now every model asset (589 files, glTF/GLB) has triangle collision, built CPU-side from the same file the renderer draws.

- **Walking:** the body stops at walls and passes doorways and window openings. Anything under 0.5 m is stepped onto, so stairs, slabs and curbs work.
  - Against real geometry the torso is 0.32 m wide, so a 1 m doorway or stairwell fits.
  - Floors and stairs are ground: you can go upstairs, stand on roofs, and fall out of upper windows.
- **Vaulting:** jumping at a waist-high obstacle with a clear gap above (a window sill, a low wall) vaults it.
- **Bullets and line of sight** use the same triangles. NPC sight uses `CombatWorld`, so bots see and shoot through open windows too.
- **Procedural houses** (Espera and the original towns) already had real door and window holes for walking. Bullets and sight now honour those holes too, instead of hitting an invisible solid box.

**Windows are knocked off.** On every one-storey wall piece, the window is removed from both collision and drawing:
- the six Building Parts window walls (brick, fancy brick and stucco, `_window_1` and `_window_2`), where windows were painted onto a panel of the shared Houses texture;
- the Downtown window parts, which had glass plus a painted "fake interior" card.

The prebuilt flats and the Downtown towers keep their glass. They are hollow shells with no floors inside, so they stay as solid cover rather than revealing an empty box. Closed-door wall pieces keep their doors; use the `*_door_frame` pieces for open doorways.

`OUTLAND_COLLISION_AUDIT=1 ./outland` (DEV build) loads every catalog model through raylib and confirms the collision mesh matches what is drawn. Result: 587 checked, 0 mismatched.

## What changed in MAP BUILDER

- **3D previews on asset cards.** They render two per frame while the drawer is open and are cached (96). Paging stays smooth.
- **Building parts can touch and stack.** The preview used to turn red whenever a piece touched another one, so walls meeting at a corner, or a second storey on a first, could never be placed. Whole objects (houses, props) are still refused when they overlap.
- **GRID cycles OFF → 1 m → 1.5 m.** At GRID 1.5, 3 m walls snap edge to edge, ROTATE (and Q/E) turns 90°, and RAISE/LOWER move one 3 m storey.
- **Joysticks and gameplay buttons** no longer draw under the open asset drawer.
- **New Parts: Kit Floor Slab 3x3 and Kit Roof Slab 3x3.** These are textured, sized to the 3 m wall modules, and generated by `tools/generate_kit_slabs.py`. Building parts now always collide, so thin slabs stop bullets.

**Downtown pack:** its 45 models reference texture PNGs that are not in the repository, so they draw white (you'll see this in the previews). If you have the original Quaternius textures, copy the `T_*.png` files next to the `.gltf` files in `assets/verda/creator/downtown/{buildings,parts,props,roads}/` and they will appear.

## The map: `maps/verda_world.map`

`outland_build_world` (DEV build) dresses the five towns with your assets and writes an ordinary Creator map. Every piece is a normal world asset that you can select, move, delete and save.

| Town | What's there |
|---|---|
| Verda (capital) | 5 enterable brick/stucco buildings (3 two-storey with stairs), 4 apartment blocks as skyline cover, street lights, traffic lights, bus stops, bollards, a barrier-and-cone checkpoint |
| Roka | Fenced industrial yard: 3 large sheds, shacks and lean-tos, pallets, barrels, barricades, dumpsters, pipes, plus a two-storey works office |
| Porto Luma | Waterfront: 3 warehouses, a quay of bollards and Jersey barriers, cargo, a two-storey harbour office |
| Suda Haveno | 4 small homes with picket-fenced gardens and bins, a bus stop, street lights |
| Espera | Drystone field walls, post-and-rail paddocks, a lean-to barn, a shack, 2 homes |

That is 15 enterable buildings and 1,681 pieces. Lots that would overlap existing buildings, streets or markers are skipped automatically. Regenerate any time with:

```sh
./build/dev-raylib6/outland_build_world        # writes maps/verda_world.map
```

### Your personal save wins

The game loads `~/.local/share/outland/maps/verda_creator.map` first, if it exists, and only then the published `maps/verda_world.map`. To start from the new towns, back up your save once:

```sh
mv ~/.local/share/outland/maps/verda_creator.map ~/.local/share/outland/maps/verda_creator.before-towns.map
```

Builder edits then autosave to a new personal save that starts from the dressed towns. EXPORT and the publish steps in `creator-builder-repair-handoff.md` still apply.

## Performance notes

Small props stop drawing at 55 m plus 12 m per metre of size (a bollard at about 60 m). Building pieces and big objects keep the 320 m range. Collision meshes load lazily and are preloaded at session start; all 589 models take about 0.3 s in an optimised build. Collision queries use a per-model grid.

The phone has not been profiled. Watch FPS in the capital, where several hundred pieces are within 320 m.

- `outland_mesh_collision_tests`: all models load; window panels removed only where expected; doorway, window, wall, closed door, low wall, rotated pieces; bullets through windows and doorways but not walls or lintels; vaults; stairs and floors; legacy box occupancy.
- `outland_world_kit_tests`: deterministic generation and save/load round trip. On every building: walk in through the front door, stand on the floor, shoot through the doorway; on every two-storey building: climb the stairs and walk around upstairs.
- `outland_creator_touch_tests` (extended): GRID 1.5, 90° turns, storey heights, and wall pieces placed touching and stacked.
- `outland_combat_tests` (updated): bullets pass procedural doorways and windows, and are stopped by walls and sills.
- DEV **37/37**, release **31/31**. ASan/UBSan clean on mesh collision, world kit, Creator touch, combat, NPC and life suites.
- The real game was run under Xvfb with Mesa: Explore, walking in through an opened window, viewing the stairs, flying over the capital, and browsing asset previews. This is not a phone test.

## Downtown Verda: a mid-sized American grid city

The capital has been wiped and rebuilt (`outland_build_world` → `maps/verda_world.map`). It follows the downtown street standards of US cities: roughly 60 ft rights-of-way with 10–18 ft sidewalks, street trees and lights in the curb zone, and signals at corners. The building stock is modelled on mid-sized downtowns: attached 2–3 storey flat-roofed brick commercial rows around a taller core.

- **Grid:** 80 m blocks (4 × 4) and ten 12 m asphalt streets. Every crossing has a crosswalk plate. Each block is a 68 m sidewalk pad 0.15 m above the street, so cars are kept off it by the curb. The four highways now meet the grid at the ends of the two main streets.
- **Core blocks:** a dark-glass bank tower (20 floors), a blue-glass office tower, an art-deco stone tower with a setback, and mid-rises. The empty quadrants are tree-lined office plazas with benches.
- **Courthouse square:** a lawn with crossing paths, a monument on a plinth, a ring of trees and a bus stop.
- **Main Street blocks:** rows of attached brick buildings in red, brown, tan, cream and dark brick. They have stone lintels and sills, cornices, sign bands and striped awnings. Every one is **enterable**:
  - storefront doors and low-sill display windows;
  - open upper windows with no glass;
  - wooden floors;
  - switchback stairs to every floor.
- **Edges:** two corner parking lots and two mixed blocks (a brick row plus a mid-rise).
- **Street life:**
  - about 200 street trees;
  - a lamp on every block face;
  - signals on the nine core crossings;
  - bus stops;
  - **16 parallel-parked hatchbacks** that you can get into and drive. The capital's own hatchback now waits in a lane.

70 buildings in Verda can be entered in all. The models come from `tools/generate_city_kit.py`. It uses the standard library only, draws every texture procedurally, and writes the Creator catalog header (`CityAssetCatalog.hpp`). CTest checks that both the city kit and the slabs are up to date. All 24 city models are in MAP BUILDER for hand placement.

**Explore** uses the new city:
- Main Street buildings are homes (flats above shops), shops, a pub, a church, police and a clinic.
- The towers are offices. Office workers go in and out through the entrance, and no body is ever simulated inside a solid tower.

**Driving fixes:**
- **Getting in:** reach is measured to the car body (2 m from a door or bumper), and things under the car no longer block the approach.
- **Escaping an overlap:** a car that starts overlapping something (a post on its spawn) gets one body length to drive clear, instead of being stuck forever.
- **Training range:** the DEV training range (grey cube and target dummies at the origin) now exists only in DEV, because the origin is downtown's main crossing.

Validation: `outland_world_kit_tests` enters every building through its door, climbs every staircase, checks that towers are solid, enters and drives all 16 parked cars plus the capital car, and checks downtown residents. `outland_vehicle_tests` covers body reach and drive-out-of-overlap.
