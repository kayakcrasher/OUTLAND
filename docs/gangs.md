# Gang wars: who runs Verda

A gangster's goal in Explore is to make their gang the biggest on the island, then the only one: every town in its colours and every ranching family riding with it or paying it. Any of the five gangs can be joined (see [careers](careers.md)); the rest fight back, and the Policio chases everyone.

Logic: `game::mafia::Territory` (turf, hold, families, tags). Tags are drawn by `game::mafia::GangLogos`. Wiring: `Renderer.cpp`.

## The five gangs

Each gang starts with every town in its county, its major city included. A crew is posted in each town's hideout.

| Gang | English | Colour | Home county | Logo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| La Verda Mano | The Green Hand | green | Verdkerno (Verda City) | an open hand, LVM |
| Montaj Lupoj | Mountain Wolves | grey | Altverdo | a wolf's head, ML |
| La Ora Ankro | The Golden Anchor | gold | Lumamaro | an anchor, OA |
| Marĉaj Ranoj | Marsh Frogs | teal | Marĉfluo | a frog, MR |
| Feraj Hundoj | Iron Dogs | rust | Ferarbaro | a spiked collar, FH |

La Verda Mano, round the capital, is the hardest crew to fight.

**Main bases:** the hideouts are fixed: the same buildings every game, the one nearest the middle of town for its kind.
- Each gang's **main base** (BAZO) is in its county's main town: Verda City, Espera, Porto Luma, Suda Haveno and Roka.
- A main base holds at least 6 men.
- The map marks each one with a ringed star and the gang's name.

**How they dress:** every gang wears its colours, so they're easy to pick out on the street (generated by `tools/generate_gang_outfits.py` and worn on Character_05's body; a crew that changes hands changes shirts).
- La Verda Mano dress sharp: each crew's boss, and the men at the Verda City door and the casino table, wear a charcoal suit with a green tie, a green pocket square and a hand pin on the lapel. The foot soldiers wear a plain white shirt with the green hand on the chest and, bigger, across the back.
- The other gangs' foot soldiers wear a shirt in the gang's colour (grey, gold, teal, rust red) with a dark band across the shoulders; their bosses a charcoal jacket with a tie and pocket square in the gang's colour.

**Bosses:** each crew's boss stands by the hideout door, his name over his head ("BOSS · gang", "USE to talk - join the gang"). In the campaign, Uncle Dario at the Green Hand's Verda City bar is on the compass for a nephew with no road yet, and his name shows from 34 m.

**Honour:** a gang that kills only other gangsters and defends its towns has broken no law (see [careers](careers.md)).

## La Verda Mano's colours

- **The banner:** La Standardo de la Verda Mano is the Ulster Banner done in Esperanto:
  - a green cross on white;
  - Esperanto's five-pointed star in white, carrying the open green hand;
  - a gold crown above it (green velvet, emeralds and pearls) with a green star on top.

  It is drawn in `game::culture::verda_mano_image`.
- **Flags:** the banner flies over every hideout La Verda Mano holds, on a staff off the wall above the door. In every town it holds, it also flies on a pole beside the national flag in the square.
  - When the gang takes a town, the banner goes up there.
  - When it loses one, the banner comes down.
- **The mural:** a gable-end mural, Belfast style, is painted on an open side wall of the Verda City hideout (`GangLogos::prepare_mural`). It has:
  - a painted frame with gold corner blocks;
  - the banner in the middle, between two flags on poles, in a laurel wreath;
  - the hand in roundels over "VERDKERNO" and "FONDITA 1887";
  - scrolls reading "LA VERDA MANO" and "NENIAM CEDI" (never yield);
  - "VERDA URBO - ĈI TIE REGAS LA VERDA MANO" at the foot.

  If the city falls, it is painted over.
- **The Verda Mano GT:** the gang's sports car is parked outside the Verda City hideout. It is the quickest car on the island (top speed 70, against the Interceptor's 60).
  - **Paint:** a low pearl-white coupe carrying the banner's cross: a green stripe nose to tail, crossed by a green band over the roof and down the doors, edged in gold. It has a wing on gold endplates.
  - **Livery:** the star, hand and crown are on the bonnet, and the banner is on both front wings (`draw_green_hand_livery`).
  - **Who drives it:** members drive it freely. Anyone else who takes it has La Verda Mano after them.
  - **Model:** `tools/generate_green_hand_car.py`.

## The ranching families

Each county has one ranch, and each ranch's family is a faction in its own right: Garza, Montoya, Villarreal, Cortez and Salazar. Their stance depends on the island's seed:

- **Allied** (two families): they ride with their county's gang. Their ranch counts for that gang. Lean on them and that gang comes after you too.
- **Fiercely independent** (two families): they fight before they pay.
- **Open** (one family): independent, but they will pay a gang that asks.

A ranch counts for a gang when the family is allied with it or paying it (**extorted**).

## Turf and hold

Every town and ranch is turf. A town has an owner and a **hold** from 0 to 100.

| What happens in a town | Hold |
|---|---|
| A rival kills one of the owner's crew | −7 |
| A rival sprays a tag | −12 |
| An AI gang raids it | −9 |
| The owner sprays a tag | +6 |
| Contraband delivered to the owner's hideout | +25 |
| Nobody contests it | +1 every 30 s |

When the hold runs out, the town goes to whichever gang pushed hardest. The hold restarts at 50, and the crew there turns to the new colours.

- **AI gangs** don't sit still. About every 2½ minutes one pushes into the nearest town held by someone else and sends a hit squad. Your gang's moves are yours to make.
- **Rival turf:** the 5 s warning near a rival hideout still applies (see careers).

## Spray tags

- Buy **spray paint** at general stores ($15), or find it in loot. Joining a gang gives 4 cans.
- Face a wall within about 4 m and press **T** (or the **TAG** touch button). One can paints your gang's logo, about 2 × 1 m.
- A tag paints over any tag within 2.5 m. Re-spraying your own tag changes nothing.
- A tag in your own town shores up the hold. A tag in a rival town wears its hold down at once, but nobody saw you do it - yet.
- Each hideout starts with its gang's tag by the door. Tags are saved.

### Street patrols

In most towns two of the crew's soldiers patrol the street in front of their hideout, walking 60 m each way and back (`game::mafia::Patrol`). A rival's fresh tag on their gang's wall doesn't stay unnoticed for long. When a patroller comes within about 26 m of one, he has seen it:
- **The first tag of yours they find:** that gang turns on you and hunts you for a while.
- **Every one after that:** it's war. The gang turns on you and sends a hit squad in a car at your nearest crew.

Kill the patrol, or tag where they don't walk, and your tags can go unseen. Patrols only walk while you're near enough to wake their town. They don't patrol while they're on a hit squad, in a heist, held or lent to you.

Someone tips off the police at the pickup: you leave with 2 stars. A delivery adds 25 hold. | 1300 | 12 |

## The gang map

For gang members, the map (**M**) becomes a gang map:

- Each turf is a circle in its holder's colour, with the holder's name and a hold bar.
- Tag dots show where the walls are painted.
- **WHO RUNS VERDA** ranks the gangs and families by towns held, then ranches.

A turf strip on the HUD shows the town you're in, who holds it and their hold. Gang news (flips, raids, gang wars) appears above the bottom panel.

## Yin and yang

Verda is in the balance between the law and your crew. The map (M) shows it as a yin-yang emblem with a ring: **LEĜO** (the law) is the share of towns the Policio has cleared, and **KRIMO** (crime) is the share your gang runs.

- **The law grows** as you play the police campaign. Verda gets safer: with every town cleared, AI gang wars and raids come further apart (up to 4 times as rare), and cleared towns stay gang-free.
- **Your crew grows** as you play the criminal questline. In your gang's towns, witnesses keep quiet: a crime there goes unreported with a chance equal to KRIMO ("Your crew's town - nobody saw a thing").

## The big hit

Late in the gang questline, once you are a **Leŭtenanto** (70 respect) *and* your gang runs at least **75% of the island's towns**, the boss may give the order no one else gets: **THE BIG HIT**, Ĉefo Marko Forta, at Verda City HQ.
- He can only be shot while that order stands, and taking him down is a crime: a record, and +30 notoriety (the gang keeps your honour).
- **The payoff:** 500 000 kronoj, flat. Your gang takes the whole island: every town (even ones the police had cleared), every crew in your colours, and every ranching family riding with you.
- **The price:** every officer on the island comes for you.
- **Afterwards:** the Chief stays dead for good. The Policio is leaderless and takes no more recruits.

## Winning

When every town is your gang's and every family rides with you or pays you, a gold **"<GANG> RULE VERDA"** banner goes up. Holding it is another matter: the police keep coming.

## Saving and dev

- Territory is saved beside the Explore profile (`<profile>.territory`): owners, holds, family stances and tags. It is saved every couple of seconds whenever something changes.
- Dev builds log the counts: `OUTLAND territory: N turfs, N tags, N docks`.
- `outland_signage_tests` checks the banner's cross, star, hand and crown; `outland_generated_green_hand_car` keeps the GT's model in step with its generator.
- `outland_territory_tests` covers hold, flips, tags painting over, extortion by stance, raids and the save. `outland_career_tests` covers the three jobs.
