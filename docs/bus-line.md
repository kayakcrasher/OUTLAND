# Linio 1 · Insula Rondo: the island bus line

Three green city buses, each with a driver at the wheel, run a loop through every town in Explore:

**Verda → Espera → Porto Luma → Suda Haveno → Sovaĝejo → Roka → Verda**

The loop is 13.3 km.

## The stops

Every town has a stop from the Transit kit (`assets/verda/transit/`, El Bolillo Duro's "Bus Stop", CC0 models):
- a shelter (`bus_stop` or `bus_stop_alt`);
- a stop sign at the kerb;
- kerb pads;
- a tall street lamp.

They stand beside a road near the town centre, built by `outland_build_world` (`bus_stops()` in `VerdaWorldKit.cpp`):
- **Verda's stop** is on the north highway just outside downtown, where a 12 m bus can pull in.
- **Placement:** each stop is nearer its own town than any other, and at least 24 m from any junction.
- **Kerb side:** each shelter is on the kerb the bus actually pulls into, the side traffic keeps to. The kit traces the line on the road graph to work out which way buses pass each stop.
- **Sign:** each shelter carries a **BUSHALTEJO · *TOWN* · LINIO 1 · INSULA RONDO** sign.
- **Map:** stops appear on the map as yellow **H** squares.
- **Map builder:** the kit pieces are in its catalog; search "transit" or "haltejo".

### Turnarounds

Espera, Porto Luma, Suda Haveno and Sovaĝejo each sit at the end of their own road, so the line has to come back the way it came. Instead of the bus spinning on the spot, the kit builds a paved **turnaround** past the stop:
- either a stem off to the side leading to a 13 m ring;
- or a turning circle where the road ends.

Turnarounds are paved like the road they leave (gravel in the backcountry). Their roads use a width no other road uses (`world::roads::Turnaround::width`), which is how the bus line recognises them.

## The buses

The bus is an original low-poly model, generated by `tools/generate_bus_model.py` (`assets/verda/vehicles/runtime/bus_body.glb`). It's built from boxes with vertex colours, so there are no textures for any build to drop. It has:
- Verda green paint, a white stripe and a white upper body;
- tinted see-through windows with seats inside;
- a lit amber destination board;
- two doors on the kerb side;
- headlights, tail lights, bumpers and wheel arches.

It's a full vehicle type (`bus` in `vehicle_manifest.tsv`): 12.3 m long, 2.55 m wide, top speed 80 km/h. It has collisions, takes bullet damage and can be wrecked.

`game::transit::BusLine` drives the line:
- **Driving:** each bus moves along the road kinematically (`VehicleSystem::glide`), with its axles following the lane, so it can never wedge on a corner.
  - Top speed is 50 km/h on the open road and 32 km/h near stops.
  - It slows for corners and eases to a stop at each shelter, then waits 12 s there.
- **Yielding:** it stops for anyone or anything in its lane: the player, residents and cars. It looks ahead its full stopping distance and halts about 2 m short.
- **Drivers:** each bus has a resident at the wheel, drawn seated (`BodyPose::seated`) and visible through the windows.
- **Spacing:** buses start evenly round the loop, about 25 minutes apart.
- **At night** they run with headlights.
- **The map** shows each bus as a green marker.
- **Leaving the line:** a wrecked bus leaves the line. A running bus can't be entered.
- **Other modes:** in Battle Royale and Zombie Survival the buses wait at Verda's depot on the north highway, where anyone can drive one.

## Riders

The buses carry people:

- **Waiting:** islanders turn up at every stop over time, about 1.4 a minute (twice that at a
  town stop). The first few stand in a queue by the shelter. Once 14 are waiting, newcomers walk
  instead.
- **Boarding:** when a bus pulls in, everyone whose stop it is gets off first, then the queue
  gets on, each heading for another stop on the line.
- **Capacity:** a bus holds 40 people, a 12 m single-decker's 32 seats plus a few standing.
  When it is full, the rest wait for the next bus.
- **On board:** riders sit two by two down the aisle and can be seen through the windows (up to
  20 drawn per bus, when the bus is near).

## Riding the bus

In Explore and the campaign, the player can ride too:

- Walk up to a bus standing at a stop and press USE. The fare is 4 kr. A full bus has no room.
- The camera follows the bus. A line at the bottom shows the next stop and how many are aboard.
- Press USE when the bus is at a stop to get off, on the shelter's side. You can't get off while
  it is moving, or shoot from the bus.

## Tests

- **`outland_bus_line_tests`** runs the line for 40 simulated minutes and checks:
  - there is a stop in every town, named for it, starting at the capital;
  - every shelter is on the bus's kerb side;
  - there are three buses with drivers;
  - every bus calls at every stop, pulling up at the kerb;
  - buses keep to the road (within 2.5 m on tight corners) and the speed limit;
  - a bus stops short of a person in its lane and goes on when they move;
  - running buses can't be entered, and a wreck leaves the line;
  - stopping the line parks the buses at the depot;
  - islanders queue, board and get off at their own stops, a bus never holds more than 40, and
    long queues walk;
  - the player gets on at a stop, can't get off while it moves, and gets off at the next stop.
- **`outland_signage_tests`:** every stop is signed, with a named Linio 1 sign in every town.
- **`outland_skeletal_gait_tests`:** the seated driver pose.
