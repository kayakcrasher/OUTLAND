# OUTLAND Training Arena

The Training Arena is OUTLAND's first playable map.

It is both a sandbox and a development environment.

---

## Phase 1

Basic environment:

- ground
- player spawn
- player movement
- first-person camera
- third-person camera
- basic collision

---

## Phase 2

Shooting range:

- firearm
- ammunition
- reload
- recoil
- projectile or hitscan testing
- target dummies
- damage
- health

---

## Phase 3

Combat training:

- AI enemy
- AI movement
- perception
- shooting
- cover
- death
- respawning

---

## Phase 4

Vehicle course:

- car
- acceleration
- steering
- braking
- collision
- vehicle damage
- enter vehicle
- exit vehicle

---

## Phase 5

Airfield:

- runway
- airplane
- flight controls
- takeoff
- landing
- aircraft damage

---

## Phase 6

Mini settlement:

- roads
- buildings
- interiors
- loot
- AI navigation
- combat encounters

---

## Long-Term Purpose

Every major OUTLAND system should be testable inside the Training
Arena before being introduced into the main open world.

The Training Arena therefore remains part of the project even after
the full OUTLAND world exists.
