Trailer Park asset pack
Creator: El Bolillo Duro
Source: https://elbolilloduro.itch.io/trailer-park

Production integration:
- Six curated trailer prefabs converted from the supplied FBX scene.
- Original archive and staging files are intentionally not committed here.

License note:
The source page advertises the models as CC0.
Some source textures may originate from third-party texture sites.
Review texture licensing separately before a public/commercial release.

Runtime preparation:
- Embedded JPEG textures re-encoded as PNG (tools/convert_glb_jpeg_to_png.py), because raylib's
  default build cannot decode JPEG. CTest outland_trailer_texture_validation keeps it that way.
- Door and screen-door nodes ("Door*") are knocked out of collision and drawing in MeshCollision so
  the trailers can be entered; insect-screen ("Mesh") panels are knocked out too (open windows).
